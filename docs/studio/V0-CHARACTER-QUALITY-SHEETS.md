gathering frame. Metrics are from the [founder audit](evidence/v0/founder-audit.json).

Cross-cutting findings:

1. All silhouettes come from 4–14 rigid primitive parts. Seams and
   interpenetration are the main Q1 tell.
2. Materials are flat colours. Roughness, metallic and emission barely vary
   within a character, and there are no textures, by design of the E1 path.
3. Faces are dot eyes on ellipsoids, with no brows, nose or expressive features.
4. Scale: the sprite and Lumi are small relative to their focus cameras, and
   framing is not subject-centred for every focus index.
5. Motion is E1's procedural kernel; all characters share its vocabulary.
